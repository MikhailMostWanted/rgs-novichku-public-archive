RGS Geographic Atlas — local resources and provenance

OFFICES / COORDINATES
Official names and addresses: https://www.rosgranstroy.ru/filialy
Central Office: https://www.rosgranstroy.ru/contacts
Checked: 2026-10-19.
The current illustration includes 14 verified building-level locations
(Central Office and 13 branches). It does not claim to show the entire network.
No street centroids, invented office locations, or estimated coordinates are used.
Four ambiguous address matches were excluded; see office-provenance.json.

Coordinates: OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0.
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
A one-time small extraction was geocoded through Nominatim, on one machine,
in a single thread, with a maximum of one request per second and cached results.
No public geocoding API is called by the website. Individual OSM object URLs,
matched house numbers, official source URLs, and exclusions ship in
public/immersive/office-provenance.json and src/immersive/office-data.json.

EARTH IMAGERY
NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble: Next Generation — July 2004 base map.
https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-map/
https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-base/july/world.200407.3x5400x2700.jpg
Credit: NASA Earth Observatory / Reto Stöckli, NASA Goddard Space Flight Center.
NASA imagery usage: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/
No NASA logo, endorsement, or current weather representation is implied.
The original 5400 x 2700 mosaic was locally downsampled to 4096 x 2048,
slightly desaturated, and compressed to WebP for realtime rendering.
The static fallback was projected from the same image and viewpoint locally.
Atmosphere, graticule geometry, lighting, geographic projection, and animation
are original procedural work for this project, not current Earth observations.

LEGACY ARTWORK

https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
